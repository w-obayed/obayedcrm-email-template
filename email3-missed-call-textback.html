Someone called. You were on another job. What happens next?
For busy HVAC contractors, missed calls are part of the day. The challenge is that people looking for service often don't leave voicemails — they move on and try the next option. A faster response, even an automated one, can make a real difference in whether they wait for you or move on.
Missed-Call Text-Back is a simple automation: when an incoming call goes unanswered, a pre-written text message is automatically sent to the caller. It acknowledges them, lets them know you'll be in touch, and gives them an easy way to continue the conversation.
What it does
How the text-back system handles missed calls:
✔ Automatic SMS acknowledgment — a text is sent to the caller within seconds of the missed call (when the system is configured to do so).
✔ Two-way conversation — the caller can reply directly, and you can pick up the conversation from your CRM when you're free.
✔ Customizable message — the text is written in your own words and can be personalized to match your business tone.
✔ Connects to appointment booking — if the lead is interested, they can self-schedule directly from the conversation.
✔ Works alongside your existing phone setup — no need to change how you receive calls.
A quicker acknowledgment, not a guarantee
Not every missed call will turn into a booked job — but reaching out quickly gives you a better chance to reconnect. The system handles the first message; you handle the relationship from there.
